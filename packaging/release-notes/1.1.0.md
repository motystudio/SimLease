# SimBar 1.1.0

After updating, open the gear menu in SimBar and choose **Install everything** to update the `sim-lease` script and the `CLAUDE.md` rule.

## Changed

- The `CLAUDE.md` rule now says plainly that it never reduces how often Claude should test on a device, and that a blocked test must be reported rather than dropped.
- A simulator booting for one session no longer holds up the other sessions.
- When the cap is reached and a session needs another model, a released device kept warm is shut down to make room.

## Fixed

- Without `--name`, iOS sessions no longer get an idle iPad or Apple Watch.
- The "all devices are in use" message no longer counts idle devices as in use.
- With every device busy, `acquire` now waits as asked instead of failing.
- A simulator shut down during its boot is no longer handed to a session.
- Android: two emulators booting at once no longer get mixed up.
